How to Play

Three rounds, one set of cards, 30 seconds at a time.

Setup

  • You only need one phone or tablet with ChatterBash installed. It gets passed to whoever is describing, so nobody else needs the app.
  • Divide into two or more teams. Team sizes don't need to match.
  • Choose which card packs to play with. Cards are drawn evenly from the packs you select. A game uses 40 cards by default, and you can change that on the setup screen.
  • Give every player a name and an avatar on the naming screen. You can add or remove teams and players there too.
  • Games save automatically, so you can pick up where you left off from Saved Games.

Turn order

  • Teams take turns in order (Team 1, Team 2, Team 1, …), and each team rotates through its own players, even if the teams are different sizes.
  • Each turn lasts 30 seconds.
  • The player whose turn it is sees one card at a time. Their teammates guess out loud.
  • Tap Got it! when your team guesses correctly. That scores 1 point and moves on to the next card.
  • In Round 1, you can tap the card to swap it for a random unused card. The timer starts again as soon as you confirm.
  • There's no skipping. If the timer runs out, the current card goes back on top of the pile for the next player.
  • Said something you shouldn't have, or need to fix how a card was scored? Tap End Turn (or let the timer run out). Then, on the Time's Up screen, give any card from that turn to whichever team should have it. Or leave it unassigned to shuffle it back into the deck.

Three rounds

You play the same set of cards three times, reshuffled between rounds. A round ends once every card has been guessed.

ROUND 1
Explaining

Describe the card using any words except the word itself.

ROUND 2
One Word

Give exactly one word as your only clue.

ROUND 3
Mime

No words at all. Act it out, and noises are allowed.

Scoring

  • 1 point for each card your team guesses.
  • Scores carry over across all three rounds.
  • The team with the most points after Round 3 wins.

Tips

  • Round 1: use stories, examples, and synonyms. The more vivid, the better.
  • Round 2: everyone remembers the hints from Round 1, so one well-chosen word can be enough.
  • Round 3: act out the memorable moments from the earlier rounds.

Setup

  • Made for two. You play together as a team, with one person describing and the other guessing, and try to beat your own best score.
  • Enter both players' names and choose your card packs.
  • Games save automatically, so you can pick up where you left off from Saved Games.

Turns

  • Take turns describing. Each turn is 30 seconds, and a round has 10 turns (5 each).
  • Tap Got it! when your partner guesses correctly, and a new card comes up.
  • In Round 1, you can tap the card to swap it for a new one. The timer starts again as soon as you confirm.
  • There's no skipping. In Round 1, the card you're holding when time runs out is discarded. In Rounds 2 and 3, it goes back on the pile for your partner.
  • Tapped Got it! by mistake, or guessed it just as the buzzer went? On the Time's Up screen, tap a card to switch it between Got it and Missed.

Three rounds

In Round 1, cards are dealt at random from your packs. Every card you guess becomes your deck for Rounds 2 and 3, reshuffled. Those rounds end after 10 turns, or sooner if you get through the whole deck.

ROUND 1
Explaining

Describe the card using any words except the word itself.

ROUND 2
One Word

Give exactly one word as your only clue.

ROUND 3
Mime

No words at all. Act it out, and noises are allowed.

Scoring

  • Your score is the number of cards you guess together.
  • The more cards you get in Round 1, the bigger your deck, and the more there is to score in Rounds 2 and 3.
  • At the end, you'll see your total, each round's score, and how many cards each of you described.

Tips

  • Round 1: use stories, examples, and synonyms. The more vivid, the better.
  • Round 2: everyone remembers the hints from Round 1, so one well-chosen word can be enough.
  • Round 3: act out the memorable moments from the earlier rounds.
