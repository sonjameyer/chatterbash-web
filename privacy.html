Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Bouncy Wizard ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ChatterBash mobile app (the "App") for iOS and Android. This policy applies to both versions of the App. This page explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and who to contact with questions.

Information we collect

ChatterBash is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not require an account, sign-in, or internet connection to play.

  • Player names and avatars. Names you type in and the avatars you pick are saved only in the App's local storage on your device, so you can reuse them next time. This information is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Game progress and settings. Saved games, card pack selections, and sound settings are likewise stored locally on your device only.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and the App does not contain advertising or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.

In-app purchases

Card packs can be purchased in the App. On iOS, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; on Android, they are processed by Google through Google Play Billing. Apple or Google handles all payment information — we never see or store your payment details. Their own privacy policies (Apple, Google) cover how they handle this data.

To unlock the packs you've bought, the App asks the App Store or Google Play to confirm which purchases belong to your store account. This check happens between your device and Apple or Google; no purchase information is sent to us.

Backups

Like most apps, the App's locally stored data may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud Backup on iOS, or Google backup on Android), depending on your device settings. These backups are managed by Apple or Google under your account, not by us.

Data deletion

Since everything the App stores lives on your device, you can remove it at any time:

  • iOS: delete the App from your Home Screen or via Settings → General → iPhone Storage → ChatterBash → Delete App.
  • Android: clear the App's storage or uninstall it via Settings → Apps → ChatterBash.

Purchases are tied to your App Store or Google Play account, so you can restore them after reinstalling.

Children's privacy

ChatterBash is a general-audience party game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what the App collects (for example, if we add analytics or online features in the future), we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
