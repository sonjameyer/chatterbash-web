How it works

Split into teams and take turns describing, hinting at, or acting out general-knowledge terms in 30-second bursts. Play the same set of cards across three rounds, each with a different twist:

ROUND 1
Explaining

Describe the term any way you like — just don't say it.

ROUND 2
One Word

One word. That's your only clue.

ROUND 3
Mime

No words at all — just actions.

Play with your own mix of packs

Start free with the General Knowledge pack, then add themed card packs — Science, Geography, Star Wars, and more — so every group can play with a different mix depending on what they own.

Built for the whole group

Teams don't need to be evenly matched — add or remove players and teams on the fly, save a game partway through, and pick up right where you left off later.
