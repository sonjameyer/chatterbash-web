Game night at home

ChatterBash runs on a single phone that gets passed around the room. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and you don't need an internet connection. Download the app, split into teams, and start playing.

Pick packs for your crowd

Mix General Knowledge with themed packs that suit the group, like Science for the nerds or Star Wars for the fans.

Keep teams flexible

Someone arriving late or leaving early? You can add or remove players and teams without restarting the game.

Take a break

Games save automatically, so grab some snacks between rounds and pick up where you left off.

Make it a night

  • Play several short games with different packs, and crown a champion team at the end of the night.
  • Mix up the teams for each new game so everyone gets to play together.
  • Agree on house rules before you start, such as whether rhyming clues count.

Host a game night at your venue

Good friends. Big laughs. Everything you need to run a night of ChatterBash at your pub, restaurant or games evening.

ChatterBash is a three-round team game played in person, using the same word bank across all three rounds, on one phone per table. As host, you don't referee every turn. You guide the room from round to round, keep the energy up, and hand out prizes at the end.

The golden rule: the point isn't to win, it's to laugh the most.

What you need

  • A screen (TV or projector) and a laptop to run the slides.
  • The Game Night slides, downloaded above.
  • A microphone, if the room is big or noisy.
  • Players: at least four per table, split into two or more teams. Teams don't need to be even.
  • One phone per table with ChatterBash installed. It's free to download from the App Store and Google Play, with no sign-up and no ads.
  • Five small prizes. A round of drinks, a dessert or a voucher works well.

Before the night

  1. Edit the cover slide with your venue name, date and start time.
  2. Play a game yourself. Download ChatterBash and play once with friends or staff, so you can answer questions on the night.
  3. Set up Presenter View. Each slide has a short host script in the speaker notes. In Presenter View, you see the script on your laptop while the room sees only the slides.
  4. Test the screen and sound before guests arrive.
  5. Promote it. A table card or social post a week before helps fill the room.

The three rounds

ROUND 1
Describe the word

One player describes the word on screen to their team, saying anything except the word itself or part of it. The team shouts guesses until time's up, then the phone passes to the next team.

ROUND 2
One word

Same idea, but the describer gets just one word per clue. No acting, pointing or sounds. Choose wisely.

ROUND 3
Charades and noises

No words at all. Players act it out, and noises are allowed (and encouraged). This is usually the loudest round of the night.

The prizes

Most of the prizes reward the funniest play, not the highest score. At the end, ask the room for nominations and let the loudest cheer decide, or make the call yourself.

Hosting tips

  • Guide, don't police. Tables play at their own pace. Your job is to start each round, check everyone is ready, and keep things moving.
  • Walk the room. You'll spot the prize-winning moments as they happen, and nominations at the end come easier.
  • Leave a short break between rounds for drinks and food orders.
  • Encourage social posting. Ask guests to tag ChatterBash on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook.
  • Ask before filming. ChatterBash produces great video moments. Get people's permission before you record or post them.
  • Make it a regular. Announce the next game night date before people leave.
